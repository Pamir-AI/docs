## Charging Tips

**Charger Compatability **

Distiller BHV edition is a battery powered distiller. To charge the device, use a compatible power delivery charger with 9V/3A capability. 

You can purchase a known to work charger [here](https://a.co/d/foocdG9).

**Charging Indication**

When the compatible charger is pluged in to the USB-C port, the on board led will turn red, indicate the device is charging. The Eink display will not show battery percentage.

## Device Low Battery

**Battery Bellow 1%**

Distiller will automatically forced shutdown when battery is bellow 1%. A info screen will be displayed. You will not be able to turn on the device while battery is bellow 3%. However, whenever a compatible charger is pluged in, device can be turned on via long button presses regardless of battery percentage.\
\
**Battery Bellow 5%**

Although a low battery warning screen will show, you can still power on your distiller device. However, it is advisable to still charge the device before attemp to turn on.
