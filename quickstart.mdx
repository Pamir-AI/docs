## Connect to WIFI

The only way for distiller to connect to WIFI (so you can ssh into it) is via VOICE, yes VOICE. 

Once the device is powered on, go to the Wifi-use mcp server

![Screenshot 2025-05-08 at 10.59.39 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-05-08at10.59.39PM.png)

Try to be patient (like how u treate a 5 years old) and guide it to connect wifi for you step by step, you can start by : \
"help me scan wifi fist"

Once you see the wifi you want to connect to listed (should be the one you PC connected to), tell it the name and password. 

once you successfully connected to the wifi (congrat, I know its very hard), you can ask the device to show you ssh instruction and it should guide you from there.
